# AI & CRISPR Research Dashboard

**Power BI portfolio project focused on scientific data analysis, KPI design, data modeling, and interactive visualization.**

This repository contains a **public, sanitized portfolio edition** of a Power BI dashboard developed in the context of undergraduate research involving Artificial Intelligence applied to CRISPR technologies.

> **Data privacy notice:** all data published in this repository are synthetic and were created exclusively for portfolio demonstration. They do **not** represent the original research sample, unpublished classifications, results, or scientific conclusions.

## Project overview

The dashboard was designed to organize and visualize structured information extracted from scientific literature on the use of Artificial Intelligence in CRISPR-related applications.

The public edition preserves the analytical structure, data model, relationships, measures, and visual logic of the original project while replacing research records and results with synthetic data.

## Data model

The public model contains six synthetic tables:

- `Articles` — central article-level analytical table
- `Architectures` — model architecture categories
- `Metrics` — evaluation metrics
- `Limitations` — methodological limitation themes
- `Interpretations` — synthetic analytical insights
- `Quality` — synthetic methodological quality indicators

`Article ID` is an artificial identifier used to preserve relationships across the public datasets.

## Data privacy and sanitization

The original research dataset is **not included** in this repository.

The portfolio edition replaces or removes:

- real article references and identifiers
- study-level extracted information
- unpublished research classifications
- methodological results
- original quality scores
- model/tool information that could reconstruct the research sample
- research-group interpretations and conclusions

The public datasets preserve the dashboard structure and analytical relationships without exposing confidential or unpublished research information.

For additional details, see [`docs/SANITIZATION.md`](docs/SANITIZATION.md) and [`docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`](docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md).
